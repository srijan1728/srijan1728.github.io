Research Interests

My research interests lie in algebraic number theory, particularly modular forms, modularity theorems, and the local and p-adic Langlands programs. I also work in computational number theory, focusing on the classification of small cyclotomic integers.

  1. With Debargha Banerjee and Kannappan Sampath, Locally algebraic lattices for inner forms of GL2: explicit construction and multiplicity one in mod p cohomology. (In preparation.)
  2. With Jitendra Bajpai, Kiran S. Kedlaya and Jorge Mello, On the classification of small cyclotomic integers. [arXiv]
  3. With Debargha Banerjee, Dhrubajyoti Das, Tathagata Mandal and Sudipa Mondal, Towards a generalized Maeda conjecture for modular forms with quadratic nebentypus. [arXiv]. (Submitted.)
